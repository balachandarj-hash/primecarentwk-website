About PrimeCare Network

Care coordination that helps hold the treatment journey together.

Personal injury care can become fragmented quickly—between providers, appointments, imaging, rehab, records, and the many people who need updates along the way. PrimeCare Network brings those moving parts together through an established Provider Network and dedicated Care Coordination.

Who we are

A network built around access, coordination, and follow-through.

PrimeCare Network helps personal injury patients, law firms, and providers stay connected as treatment moves forward. We do not replace the treating provider, and we do not manage the legal case. We coordinate the administrative pathway that helps care continue with fewer gaps.

Our team helps coordinate:

  • Provider access
  • Appointments
  • Referrals
  • Imaging
  • Procedures
  • Rehab
  • Notifications and reminders
  • Status updates
  • Medical records
  • Cost estimates
  • Reductions when applicable
  • Communication across stakeholders

The result is a clearer, easier pathway from first referral through completion of treatment.

Why PrimeCare exists

Because care can become complicated long before the legal case does.

After an injury, treatment often involves multiple offices, scheduling windows, and stakeholders who all need timely information. Without coordination, small gaps can slow recovery and cloud case visibility.

Complications can include:

  • Delayed scheduling
  • Missed appointments
  • Unclear next steps after imaging or specialist visits
  • Gaps between referrals
  • Incomplete or slow medical records
  • Limited visibility into treatment status
  • Fragmented communication between patients, providers, and counsel

PrimeCare exists to bring continuity and follow-through to that journey—so care can keep moving with fewer drop-offs.

Independent care. Connected coordination.

We coordinate access. Treating professionals provide the care.

PrimeCare Network coordinates access to independent providers and professionals. PrimeCare does not itself provide medical services. Treating professionals remain responsible for clinical care and medical decision-making. Patients decide on treatment with their providers. Attorneys manage the legal case. PrimeCare connects the administrative journey that helps hold those pieces together.

Our commitment

Make access easier. Keep communication moving. Follow through.

Every personal injury case involves people who need clarity at different moments—a patient waiting for the next appointment, a provider waiting on records or confirmations, a law firm waiting on status. Our commitment is to reduce friction at each of those points.

We work to make provider access easier, keep communication moving across stakeholders, and follow through as treatment progresses—so the pathway from referral to completion of care is clearer and more connected.

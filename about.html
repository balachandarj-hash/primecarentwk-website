About PrimeCare Network

Care that holds the case together.

We exist to make personal injury recovery less fragmented—for patients healing, providers treating, and attorneys building the claim.

Our shift in focus

Network. Coordination. Clinical case management.

Financing has always been part of how PrimeCare removes barriers to treatment. Today, our identity leads with what makes those outcomes possible: a nationwide providers network, dedicated care coordination, and legal nurse case managers who keep the medical story accurate and actionable.

Lien financing and pre-settlement support remain available—positioned as enabling tools, not the whole story.

Why partners choose us

Seamless care across a complicated journey.

  • 02

Two core pillars

The right access. The right coordination.

Personal injury treatment rarely happens in one place. A patient may need an initial evaluation, diagnostic imaging, specialty care, therapy, procedures, follow-up treatment, medical records, cost estimates, and ongoing communication between multiple offices. PrimeCare helps connect those pieces through two core pillars.

01 · Provider Network

Connect patients with the care and resources they need.

PrimeCare provides access to an established network of healthcare providers, facilities, and professional resources experienced in personal injury cases. Our team helps identify appropriate network options based on the patient’s needs, injury, location, and requested services.

PrimeCare coordinates access to independent providers and professionals. PrimeCare does not itself render the medical, clinical, diagnostic, rehabilitation, or professional services listed on this site.

How a case moves through PrimeCare

From referral to completion of care.

  1. 01

    Connect

    An attorney, patient, or participating provider contacts PrimeCare with the patient’s treatment needs. Our team reviews the request and identifies appropriate network resources and the right coordination pathway.

    Send Us a Case
  2. 02

    Coordinate

    PrimeCare assists with provider access, appointments, imaging, specialists, procedures, and other treatment-related logistics. Patients receive appointment information and automated reminders designed to help them stay informed about upcoming care.

    Start Coordination
  3. 03

    Track

    Our team helps monitor the administrative progression of care—appointment status, upcoming appointments, provider communications, medical record requests, cost estimates, and other coordination items—through one centralized process.

  4. 04

    Follow through

    When something stalls, our coordination team helps identify the issue and works with the appropriate party to move the process forward—rescheduling, pending referrals, imaging, records, cost estimates, and status updates.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    As treatment progresses or concludes, PrimeCare helps coordinate remaining administrative needs—outstanding records, cost estimates, treatment status information, and reduction coordination.

Who care coordination serves

Different needs. One connected process.

PrimeCare sits between the moving parts of personal injury care so patients, law firms, and providers do not have to manage every handoff separately.

Each party has different responsibilities, but they all benefit from clearer communication, better follow-through, and a central point of contact.

For patients

More guidance. Fewer unanswered questions.

After an injury, patients may be dealing with pain, work disruption, transportation challenges, family responsibilities, and an unfamiliar treatment process—all while trying to keep track of multiple providers and appointments.

PrimeCare helps make that process easier to navigate.

Our coordination team helps patients stay informed about where they are in the scheduling process, when appointments are coming up, and what administrative step is happening next.

Patients may receive support with:

  • Scheduling initial and follow-up appointments
  • Coordinating specialist visits
  • Coordinating imaging, testing, therapy, and procedures
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Automatic reminders for upcoming appointments
  • Future appointment notifications
  • Appointment changes and rescheduling
  • Follow-up after missed or cancelled appointments
  • Coordination when a treating provider recommends another service or specialist
  • A central point of contact for scheduling and coordination questions

The patient should not have to figure out the entire treatment pathway alone. PrimeCare helps keep patients informed, connected, and prepared for what comes next.

When care changes

We help coordinate what comes next.

Treatment rarely follows a straight line. A patient’s needs may evolve over time.

An initial evaluation may lead to imaging. Imaging may lead to a specialist. A specialist may recommend additional testing, therapy, a procedure, or another level of care.

PrimeCare helps coordinate those transitions when requested or recommended by the appropriate treating provider.

When something stalls

We help identify the administrative gap and follow through.

Care can slow down for simple reasons:

  • A referral was never scheduled
  • A patient missed an appointment
  • An office has not confirmed availability
  • Imaging is still pending
  • Records have not been received
  • A cost estimate remains outstanding
  • A follow-up appointment has not been scheduled
  • A communication needs a response

PrimeCare helps identify outstanding coordination items and works with the appropriate party to move the administrative process forward.

That follow-through may include:

  • Rescheduling an appointment
  • Following up on a pending referral
  • Confirming whether an appointment was completed
  • Contacting a provider office regarding outstanding scheduling
  • Following up on medical records
  • Following up on cost estimates
  • Confirming future appointments
  • Communicating relevant status updates to authorized parties

Important distinction

Care coordination, not medical decision-making

PrimeCare coordinates access, scheduling, communication, and other administrative aspects of the treatment journey.

PrimeCare does not diagnose patients, prescribe treatment, determine medical necessity, or replace the independent judgment of treating healthcare professionals.

Medical decisions remain between the patient and the patient’s healthcare providers.

Our role is to help make the path between those decisions easier to navigate.

Care Coordination

One dedicated case concierge. End-to-end scheduling, specialist routing, and stakeholder communication—so recovery never stalls between appointments.

Single point of contact

We hold the thread between patient, provider, and counsel.

Personal injury recovery often means multiple specialists, frequent follow-ups, and constant updates for the legal team. PrimeCare care coordinators manage that complexity as the case’s central hub.

Patients get guided access to treatment. Providers get clearer handoffs. Attorneys get timely clinical status without chasing every office.

Who coordination serves

Same coordinator. Three audiences. Shared clarity.

For patients

We schedule care across providers, help navigate next steps, and keep living- and medical-expense support options in view when financing is needed.

For providers

Coordinators interface with your practice, align treatment sequencing, and connect lien financing or management so clinical teams can stay focused on care.

For attorneys

Law firms get faster treatment access for plaintiffs, streamlined updates, and a single contact who knows the medical timeline of the case.

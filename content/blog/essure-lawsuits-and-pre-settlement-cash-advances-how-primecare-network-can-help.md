**Thousands of Lawsuits Filed Against Essure**  
Thousands of lawsuits have been filed against Essure, a medical device intended to prevent pregnancy, as women nationwide await verdicts in their favor.

In response, Bayer announced a substantial settlement to resolve most claims related to the device. The Essure settlement aims to address the majority of lawsuits brought against the company.

### **What is the Essure Device?**

Essure, a birth control device manufactured by Bayer AG under its subsidiary Conceptus, has been linked to severe health complications in women. Soon after its release, many women reported experiencing serious side effects.

Essure is a permanent birth control device inserted into the fallopian tubes to prevent conception. It received approval from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in November 2002 following clinical trials.

### **The Essure Procedure**

Marketed as a less invasive alternative to tubal ligation, the Essure procedure was a quick, 10-minute outpatient treatment. The device consisted of coils inserted into the fallopian tubes. Over three months, tissue would grow around the coils, creating a permanent barrier to prevent pregnancy.

### **Essure Injuries and Complications**

Although initial follow-up studies deemed Essure safe, mounting evidence revealed significant adverse events:

  * **Perforations:** Fallopian tube perforations caused severe complications.
  * **Persistent Pain and Bleeding:** Women reported ongoing abdominal pain and heavy bleeding.
  * **Unintended Pregnancies:** Many women became pregnant despite using the device.
  * **Fatalities:** Some deaths were linked to the device.

By 2013, thousands of women had come forward with severe side effects, leading many to have the device removed. Some required hysterectomies to alleviate chronic pain caused by the device breaking loose or piercing tissue.

### **The Beginning of Essure Lawsuits**

Litigants filed lawsuits claiming Bayer failed to disclose Essure's severe side effects and conducted inadequate testing before FDA approval.

#### **Claims in the Lawsuits**

  * Patients were not adequately informed about risks.
  * Allegations of concealed information during the FDA approval process.
  * Short-term clinical trials failed to identify long-term complications.

Women affected by Essure argued that had they been fully informed of its risks, they would have chosen alternative birth control methods.

### **FDA Interventions for Essure**

The FDA required Bayer to add a black box warning label highlighting the device's risks and implement a checklist for patients and physicians to review potential complications. Despite these measures, adverse event reports surged, with nearly 10,000 complaints received by the FDA, including at least two confirmed fatalities.

### **Essure Production Halted and Settlement Reached**

In 2018, Bayer ceased selling Essure in the U.S., having already discontinued it in other countries. Thousands of lawsuits culminated in a $1.6 billion settlement in 2020, aimed at compensating individuals and class-action litigants.

Connecting an injured patient with an appropriate provider is essential. Keeping that care pathway organized is what helps the case move forward.

## Network access with personal injury experience

PrimeCare maintains access to an established network of independent healthcare providers, facilities, diagnostics, rehabilitation resources, and professional resources experienced in personal injury matters.

That may include injury-focused medical care, orthopedics, neurology, imaging, therapy, and other specialty resources based on the needs of the case.

## After the referral

Once care begins, administrative activity multiplies: follow-ups, additional referrals, records, estimates, and status updates. PrimeCare’s coordination team helps keep those moving pieces connected.

## Built for attorneys, patients, and providers

- **Law firms** gain clearer visibility without calling every office.
- **Patients** receive scheduling support and appointment reminders.
- **Providers** receive more organized referral and administrative communication.

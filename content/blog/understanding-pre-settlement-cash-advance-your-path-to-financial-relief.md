When you're involved in a legal claim, waiting for your settlement can be stressful, especially if you're struggling with expenses.

A pre-settlement cash advance can be a lifeline, providing you with the funds you need while your case is still pending. This unique financial solution allows you to receive cash upfront based on the potential value of your lawsuit, without the burden of regular payments or interest typically associated with loans.

In this blog, we’ll explore how pre-settlement funding differs from a traditional loan, the types of cases that qualify for pre-settlement funding, and how you can use these funds to cover your immediate expenses.

### How Pre-Settlement Funding Differs from a Loan

When comparing loans to pre-settlement funding, there are key differences that can help you decide which is right for your situation:

  1. **How Much You Need:**

     * **Loans:** Typically based on your creditworthiness, income, and other financial factors.
     * **Pre-Settlement Funding:** Determined by the strength and potential settlement of your legal case, not your personal financial situation.
  2. **Pre-Qualification:**

     * **Loans:** Involves an assessment of income, credit, and financial history.
     * **Pre-Settlement Funding:** Focuses on the potential value of your case. Income or credit history usually doesn't affect approval.
  3. **Documentation:**

     * **Loans:** Requires extensive documentation, including proof of income and bank statements.
     * **Pre-Settlement Funding:** Only basic case-related documentation is needed, often without the need for detailed financial records.
  4. **Approval Process:**

     * **Loans:** Dependent on financial stability, income, and credit score.
     * **Pre-Settlement Funding:** Based on the strength of your case, with approval typically not impacted by your financial status.
  5. **Repayment:**

     * **Loans:** Requires regular payments, often with interest.
     * **Pre-Settlement Funding:** Considered non-recourse, meaning repayment is contingent on the outcome of your case. If your case doesn't settle, you owe nothing.

### Types of Cases That Qualify for Pre-Settlement Funding

Pre-settlement funding is available for a wide range of legal cases where there is a pending lawsuit and a potential settlement. These include:

  * **Personal Injury Cases:** Car accidents, slip and falls, and workplace injuries.
  * **Employment Disputes:** Wrongful termination, discrimination, and harassment.
  * **Premises Liability:** Injuries caused by unsafe conditions on property.
  * **Civil Rights Violations:** Cases involving police misconduct or constitutional rights violations.
  * **Wrongful Death Cases:** Lawsuits related to fatalities due to negligence or intentional harm.

If your case falls into one of these categories, pre-settlement funding might be an excellent option to help manage your financial obligations while waiting for your settlement.

### How You Can Use Pre-Settlement Funds

Once approved, the funds from your pre-settlement cash advance can be used for various essential expenses, including:

  * **Living Expenses:** Rent, utilities, groceries, and daily necessities.
  * **Medical Bills:** Ongoing medical treatment and related expenses.
  * **Transportation Costs:** Travel to medical appointments or court appearances.
  * **Childcare:** Daycare or babysitting services for your children.
  * **Debt Repayment:** Pay off credit card bills or other debts accumulated during your case.
  * **Emergency Expenses:** Unexpected costs like medical emergencies or urgent repairs.

Pre-settlement funding can relieve the financial stress while you focus on your recovery and case resolution.

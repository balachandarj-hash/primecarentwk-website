### Key Highlights:

  1. **Definition and Distinction** :

     * **Pre-settlement funding** is a non-recourse financial option for personal injury plaintiffs, meaning repayment is only required upon case success.
     * Differs from traditional loans as there’s no obligation if the case is unsuccessful.
  2. **Process Overview** :

     * **Application** : Applicants must submit accident-related documents and consult with their attorney.
     * **Approval** : Based on case merits, not credit scores.
     * **Disbursement and Repayment** : Funds are typically disbursed quickly, with repayment required only upon settlement.
  3. **Missouri Laws and Context** :

     * The **pure comparative fault** system in Missouri allows compensation even when plaintiffs are partially at fault.
     * Auto accidents, including car, motorcycle, and semi-truck incidents, are common in cities like Kansas City and St. Louis.
  4. **Types of Covered Accidents** :

     * Funding is available for motorized vehicle accidents but excludes non-motorized and watercraft-related incidents.

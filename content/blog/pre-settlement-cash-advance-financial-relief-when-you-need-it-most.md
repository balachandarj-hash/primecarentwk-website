**Pre-Settlement Cash Advance**

Pre-settlement funding, also known as consumer legal funding, offers a vital financial lifeline to individuals awaiting lawsuit settlements.

This service provides immediate funds to cover essential expenses during the legal process in exchange for a portion of the future settlement proceeds. Unlike traditional loans, pre-settlement cash advances are non-recourse—if your case doesn't result in financial recovery, you owe nothing back. With no monthly payments or risk of collection actions, repayment is only required if you win your case, directly deducted from your settlement funds. This option can be invaluable for those experiencing financial strain while waiting for their case to resolve.

### Who May Qualify for Pre-Settlement Cash Advances?

Often misnamed as a lawsuit settlement loan, pre-settlement funding is available for cases with expected financial settlements or judgments. This makes it an excellent choice for plaintiffs enduring lengthy legal processes with substantial expenses. Common qualifying cases include:

  * **Personal Injury Claims** – Including car accidents, slip-and-fall incidents, and workplace injuries.
  * **Employment Disputes** – Such as wrongful termination, workplace harassment, and discrimination cases.
  * **Premises Liability Cases** – Injuries from unsafe property conditions like broken stairs or unsecured walkways.
  * **Civil Rights Violations** – Cases involving police misconduct, unlawful arrests, or excessive force.
  * **Wrongful Death Cases** – Filed by families due to negligent or intentional harm, such as fatal car accidents or medical malpractice.

Pre-settlement cash advances offer critical financial support, enabling plaintiffs to meet their immediate needs while awaiting a case resolution.

### Pre-Settlement Funding for Car Accident Lawsuits

Car accident cases are among the most common applications for pre-settlement funding. This service provides a flexible alternative to loans, requiring repayment only if your case reaches a favorable settlement. This relief helps you manage medical costs and financial pressures related to your car accident injuries with peace of mind.

#### Injuries That May Qualify

Typical car accident injuries that qualify include:

  * Whiplash
  * Fractures and broken bones
  * Dislocated joints
  * Lacerations
  * Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  * Concussions
  * Heart-related injuries such as heart attacks

#### Key Documentation for a Strong Case

Having the right documentation strengthens your car accident claim, ensuring a smoother claims process:

  * Accident scene evidence (photos, videos, witness contact information)
  * Police reports
  * Medical records and bills
  * Insurance policy details
  * Proof of lost income
  * Property damage estimates
  * Documented communications and admissions of fault
  * Expert witness statements

### The Pre-Settlement Cash Advance Process

**1\. Application**  
Plaintiffs represented by an attorney can apply online. Case eligibility and potential funding depend on the case's strength and settlement value.

**2\. Case Evaluation**  
The funding provider works with your attorney to review case details and determine eligibility. Strong cases with high settlement potential are more likely to be approved.

**3\. Approval**  
Funding decisions are typically made within 24 to 48 hours.

**4\. Agreement**  
Upon approval, the plaintiff and their attorney sign an agreement detailing payment terms. Funds are released promptly upon securing signatures.

**5\. Receipt of Funds**  
Funds are usually accessible within 24–48 hours through direct deposit, check, or Western Union.

### How Pre-Settlement Funding Can Be Used

Pre-settlement funding can address various urgent expenses, including:

  * **Living Costs** – Rent, groceries, and daily necessities.
  * **Medical Expenses** – Hospital bills, prescriptions, and other healthcare costs.
  * **Household Bills** – Utility payments like electricity and water.
  * **Legal Costs** – Attorney fees or other case-related expenses.
  * **Childcare & Education** – Services and educational expenses.
  * **Transportation** – Commuting or travel costs.
  * **Emergencies** – Unplanned financial needs.

This funding relieves financial stress, allowing you to focus on recovery and legal matters.

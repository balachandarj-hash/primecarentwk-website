A pre-settlement cash advance for workers' compensation and workplace negligence claims offers immediate financial relief while waiting for your lawsuit settlement. Unlike a traditional loan, a pre-settlement advance is **non-recourse** , meaning you don’t need to repay the funds if your case isn’t successful. This funding is based on the anticipated award of your lawsuit and doesn’t require income verification or collateral.

Workers’ compensation and workplace negligence cases often take months or even years to resolve, causing financial strain due to mounting medical bills, living expenses, and other obligations. With a pre-settlement advance, you can access the money you need now to cover your essential expenses, allowing you to focus on your case without worrying about financial hardships. And, if your case doesn’t win, you’re not required to repay the funding.

### Do You Need Pre-Settlement Funding for Workers’ Comp or Workplace Negligence?

Pre-settlement funding helps alleviate financial stress by covering the costs of everyday expenses like utility bills, medical care, and living costs during the duration of your case. As workers’ compensation claims can be lengthy and unpredictable, this funding option is designed to provide a financial cushion until your case is settled.

### How Pre-Settlement Funding Works

The process of obtaining pre-settlement funding is straightforward. Here’s how it works:

  1. **Apply for Funding** : The application process is simple and doesn’t require verification of employment status.
  2. **Approval and Funding** : Once your application is approved, you’ll receive the funds quickly, with no upfront costs.
  3. **Repayment** : You only need to repay the funding if your case is successful. If you don’t win, you are under no obligation to repay the amount advanced.

### Workers’ Comp vs. Workplace Negligence

  * **Workers’ Compensation** : This system provides benefits for work-related injuries or illnesses regardless of fault. For example, a worker injured in a slip and fall accident at work would likely file a workers’ comp claim.
  * **Workplace Negligence** : In this case, the claimant must prove that the employer or another party was at fault for the injury or illness. If a worker is hit by a delivery truck while on the job and the driver’s negligence is determined to be the cause, this would fall under workplace negligence.

### Common Injuries Covered by Workers' Comp

Workers' compensation covers injuries like back strains, fractures, repetitive motion injuries, and occupational illnesses. If you suffer from any work-related injury or illness, you may be eligible for pre-settlement funding.

### Common Incidents in Workplace Negligence

Workplace negligence may include injuries caused by faulty equipment, slip and fall accidents, exposure to hazardous materials, or the lack of proper safety measures. These cases require proving that someone else’s negligence led to your injury or illness, unlike workers' comp claims, which are no-fault.

### Funding Limits and Multiple Rounds of Funding

The amount of pre-settlement funding you can receive depends on factors like:

  * The strength of your case
  * The potential settlement amount
  * Current and future medical expenses
  * Lost wages and insurance coverage
  * Case duration

You may also be eligible for additional rounds of funding if your case takes longer than expected or if your financial needs change.

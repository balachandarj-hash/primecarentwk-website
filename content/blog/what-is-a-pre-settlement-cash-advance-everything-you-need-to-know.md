### What Is a Pre-Settlement Cash Advance?

A **pre-settlement cash advance** is financial support provided to individuals involved in a personal injury lawsuit. Unlike a traditional loan, this is a **non-recourse advance** —you only repay it if you win or settle your case. If you lose, you owe nothing.

This allows you to cover urgent expenses—like rent, utilities, groceries, or medical bills—without being pressured into accepting a lower settlement.

### Who Benefits from Pre-Settlement Funding?

Anyone with a valid personal injury claim who is facing financial difficulties can apply. This includes:

  * Car or truck accident victims

  * Slip & fall injury victims

  * Victims of workplace or construction accidents

  * Families pursuing wrongful death claims

  * Individuals injured due to general negligence

For example, if you were injured in a car accident and your case is taking months or even years to resolve, a pre-settlement advance can help cover the costs of living while you wait for your settlement.

If you’ve been injured due to someone else’s negligence, you may be entitled to compensation through a personal injury lawsuit.

However, the process of filing and winning a lawsuit can take months or even years. During this time, the financial strain of medical bills, lost wages, and other living expenses can become overwhelming, especially if you’re unable to work due to your injuries.

While many personal injury cases are settled outside of court, the process of reaching a fair settlement can be lengthy. To ease the financial burden while waiting for your case to be resolved, you can consider applying for a pre-settlement cash advance. This option allows you to access funds to cover your routine expenses, such as medical bills, rent, utilities, and other necessary costs.

**How Does Pre-Settlement Funding Work?**

While the term "accident loans" is often used, pre-settlement funding is technically not a loan in most cases. Instead, it’s a cash advance based on your potential settlement. To qualify for pre-settlement funding, you need to have a pending personal injury case and be represented by an attorney. Once you’ve applied, a funding company will evaluate the strength of your case and determine how much funding you qualify for. If approved, the funds will be disbursed to you, helping you cover medical bills, living expenses, and other urgent needs while you wait for your settlement.

**Am I Responsible for Repayment if I Lose My Case?**

Pre-settlement funding is generally considered "non-recourse," meaning you don’t have to repay the money if you lose your case. However, if you win or settle, the funding company will receive its share from the settlement proceeds. If the settlement amount is less than expected, the terms of the agreement will dictate the repayment process.

**When Should You Consider Pre-Settlement Funding?**

Pre-settlement funding is especially helpful if you’ve been injured in a vehicle accident or on public property, and you’re struggling with medical bills, living expenses, or other financial pressures. It provides temporary financial relief while you await your final settlement or trial verdict. If you’re dealing with financial difficulties due to an injury, pre-settlement funding could help you manage your immediate expenses.

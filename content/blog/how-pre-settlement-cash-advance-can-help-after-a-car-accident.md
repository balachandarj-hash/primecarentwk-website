After a car accident, the financial stress can quickly pile up—medical bills, vehicle repairs, and other unexpected expenses.

While you wait for your lawsuit to settle, you may find it hard to keep up with these mounting costs. This is where a pre-settlement cash advance can offer much-needed financial relief. Unlike traditional loans, pre-settlement funding allows you to access a portion of your expected settlement before the final outcome, helping you cover immediate expenses without the added burden of repayments if the case doesn’t win.

### Who is Eligible for Pre-Settlement Funding?

If you’ve been involved in a car accident and have an active legal claim with legal representation, you could qualify for pre-settlement funding. This is particularly helpful when dealing with the financial challenges of recovering from an accident, as it can cover medical costs, lost wages, or property damage while your case is still in progress.

### How Does Pre-Settlement Funding Work?

The process for securing a pre-settlement cash advance is simple and fast. First, submit an easy application, and the funding company will evaluate your case to determine the strength of your claim. If approved, you'll receive funds typically within 24 to 48 hours. Since pre-settlement funding is non-recourse, repayment is only required if your case is successful, ensuring no financial strain if the outcome isn't in your favor.

### Types of Car Accidents Covered

Pre-settlement funding is available for a variety of car accidents, including those involving injuries to passengers and severe vehicle damage. Whether you've been rear-ended or are dealing with a multi-vehicle collision, you can qualify for funding to ease the financial burdens that come with such situations.

### Types of Injuries Covered

If you’ve suffered injuries from a car accident, pre-settlement funding may be able to assist you. Common injuries eligible for funding include:

  * Whiplash
  * Broken bones
  * Fractures
  * Concussions
  * Lacerations
  * Dislocated joints
  * PTSD

### The Pre-Settlement Funding Process

Getting pre-settlement funding is easy and fast. Here’s how it works:

  1. **Apply for Pre-Settlement Funding**  
Speak with your attorney to ensure this option is right for your case, and then apply online. Eligibility depends on the strength of your claim and your attorney’s input.

  2. **Case Evaluation**  
The funding company will assess your case in consultation with your attorney to determine your eligibility and potential funding amount.

  3. **Approval**  
Once your application is approved, the funding company will send the necessary documents for you and your attorney to sign.

  4. **Funding**  
Upon agreement, the funds are disbursed quickly, often on the same day, through direct deposit, check, or via Western Union.

### What Do You Need for a Successful Car Accident Claim?

To maximize your chances of approval for pre-settlement funding, ensure you have the following documents ready:

  * Accident scene information (photos, contact details, witness statements)
  * Police reports
  * Medical records and bills
  * Proof of lost wages
  * Property damage estimates
  * Insurance documents

### Is There a Pre-Settlement Limit?

The amount you qualify for depends on several factors such as the strength of your case, the severity of injuries, and potential settlement amounts. Your pre-settlement funding limit will be based on a comprehensive evaluation of your case.

### Can You Receive More Than One Pre-Settlement Disbursement?

In some cases, you may be eligible for more than one pre-settlement funding disbursement. If your case lasts longer than expected or if your financial needs change, additional funding may be provided based on your specific situation.

### Pre-Settlement Funding FAQs

  * **What are the risks?**  
Pre-settlement funding is non-recourse, so there is no risk of repayment if you lose the case.

  * **What is the probability of defaulting?**  
There is no risk of default since repayment is only required if you win your case.

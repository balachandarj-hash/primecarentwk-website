## Workers’ Compensation Laws in South Carolina

Understanding South Carolina’s workers’ compensation laws is critical for navigating your claim:

  * **Statute of Limitations** : Injuries must be reported within 90 days, and claims filed within two years.
  * **Medical Care** : Treatment must be received from an employer-approved provider.
  * **Benefits** : Covers medical expenses, rehabilitation, and a portion of lost wages (typically 66.67% of weekly earnings).
  * **Protection from Retaliation** : Employers cannot retaliate against you for filing a claim.

Personal injury treatment rarely stays in one office. A patient may need an evaluation, imaging, specialty care, therapy, procedures, records, and cost estimates—often across multiple locations.

Without a central coordination point, law firms and patients spend time chasing updates while appointments, referrals, and records fall through the cracks.

## What coordination actually covers

PrimeCare helps organize the administrative path around care, including:

- Appointment scheduling and status tracking
- Specialist and imaging coordination
- Patient confirmations and reminders
- Medical record and cost estimate requests
- Follow-through when something stalls

## Independent care, connected process

Treating providers remain responsible for clinical decisions. PrimeCare’s role is to connect the administrative steps so patients, providers, and law firms share clearer visibility from referral through completion of care.

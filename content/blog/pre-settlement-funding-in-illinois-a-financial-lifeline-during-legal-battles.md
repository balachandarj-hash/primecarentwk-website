Are you involved in a personal injury lawsuit in Illinois? If so, the financial strain of ongoing legal expenses, medical bills, and daily living costs may be overwhelming.

Pre-settlement funding can provide the relief you need, offering financial support without the burdens of traditional loans.

### **What Is Pre-Settlement Funding?**

Pre-settlement funding is a cash advance based on the potential outcome of your personal injury lawsuit. Unlike loans, it’s non-recourse, meaning repayment is only required if you win your case. This feature eliminates financial risk for plaintiffs, making it a safer alternative to loans that demand repayment regardless of case outcomes.

Key benefits of pre-settlement funding:

  * **No monthly payments:** The funding company recoups their investment from your settlement.

  * **No credit checks:** Approval is based solely on the merits of your case.

  * **No repayment if you lose:** You’re not obligated to pay back the funds if your case doesn’t settle favorably.

### **How Pre-Settlement Funding Works in Illinois**

Pre-settlement funding in Illinois supports plaintiffs in cases like car accidents, premises liability, and workplace negligence. Here’s how it works:

  1. **Requesting the Funding**  
Gather relevant documents such as police reports, medical records, and proof of lost wages. The funding company evaluates your case based on the strength of your lawsuit and the potential settlement amount.

  2. **Receiving the Funds**  
Upon approval, funds are typically dispersed within 24-48 hours, providing immediate financial relief. These funds can cover rent, groceries, medical expenses, and other daily costs.

  3. **Non-Recourse Funding**  
This unique structure shifts the financial risk from you to the funding company. If your case doesn’t result in a settlement, you owe nothing.

### **Common Types of Pre-Settlement Funding Cases in Illinois**

#### **1\. Car Accident Lawsuits**

Illinois sees thousands of car accidents annually, especially in cities like Chicago. Injuries from whiplash, broken bones, and severe trauma often lead to lengthy legal battles. Pre-settlement funding bridges the financial gap while victims wait for a resolution.

#### **2\. Premises Liability Lawsuits**

Premises liability, often called slip-and-fall cases, involves injuries due to unsafe property conditions. Common scenarios include:

  * Slipping on an unmarked wet floor in a grocery store.

  * Tripping over loose wiring in an office building.

Eligible injuries for funding include fractures, lacerations, and severe trauma.

#### **3\. Workplace Negligence Cases**

Workplace negligence occurs when employers fail to maintain safe conditions, causing injuries like falls from unsecured ladders or inadequate safety gear. Unlike workers’ compensation claims, these lawsuits often take longer to settle, making financial support essential.

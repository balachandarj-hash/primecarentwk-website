**Understanding Premises Liability and Slip & Fall Lawsuits**

Premises liability refers to situations where a person is injured on someone else’s property due to unsafe conditions. A slip and fall injury could occur from wet floors, poor lighting, broken stairs, or other hazardous conditions. To win a premises liability case, the injured party must prove the property owner’s negligence in maintaining the property, directly linking the unsafe condition to their injury.

### Premises Negligence (Slip & Fall)

Premises negligence occurs when a property owner or manager fails to maintain safe conditions on their property, leading to an accident, such as a slip or fall. These accidents can happen in public spaces like stores, restaurants, or private homes due to hazards like wet floors, uneven surfaces, or inadequate lighting.

If you’ve been injured due to unsafe conditions on someone else’s property, whether it’s a slip and fall at a grocery store or a trip at a poorly maintained public space, you may have grounds for a premises negligence claim and qualify for [slip and fall pre-settlement funding](</medical-care-financing-news>). However, these cases often take time to settle as property owners or insurance companies may attempt to delay or dispute responsibility.

Pre-settlement funding can help you get the financial relief you need while waiting for your lawsuit to be resolved. It can cover things like medical bills, lost wages, and other immediate expenses, giving you some peace of mind during a stressful period.

**Injuries from slip and fall accidents are often painful and can include:**

  * Sprains and strains
  * Fractures
  * Head injuries
  * Back and spinal injuries
  * Cuts and bruises
  * Knee injuries

**To build a successful premises negligence case, gather the following:**

  * Incident report (filed with the property owner or manager)
  * Photographs (of the hazard that caused the fall and the area surrounding it)
  * Medical records (showing treatment for injuries)
  * Witness statements
  * Surveillance footage (if available)
  * Maintenance records (to prove the property owner’s negligence)

### Workplace Negligence

Workplace negligence refers to a situation where an employer fails to provide a safe working environment, resulting in injury. This can involve unsafe equipment, inadequate training, lack of proper safety measures, or failure to address hazards in the workplace.

When an employer fails to provide a safe working environment or violates regulations that protect employees, workers can file a lawsuit under workplace negligence. Because workplace negligence cases can be complex and often involve multiple parties, they can take time to settle. Pre-settlement funding can help you manage your expenses, including medical bills and any lost wages, while you wait for your case to resolve.

**Common injuries from workplace negligence include:**

  * Lacerations

  * Respiratory issues (due to inadequate ventilation or exposure to toxic substances)
  * Fractures and sprains
  * Electrocution
  * Crush injuries

**To strengthen your workplace negligence claim, gather the following:**

  * Incident report (filed with the employer or HR)
  * Medical records (documenting the injuries and treatment)
  * Photos of injuries (if applicable)
  * Safety violation reports (evidence of workplace hazards)
  * Witness statements
  * Employer safety records (proving the employer’s failure to provide proper safety measures)

## The Pre-Settlement Funding Process

The entire process of pre-settlement funding is quick and easy. It consists of the following steps:

### 1\. Applying for Pre-Settlement Funding

To be eligible for pre-settlement funding, you need to be a personal injury accident victim with an active legal claim and have legal representation. If you meet these criteria, you can submit an application directly through a funding company’s website. Approval typically hinges on the strength of your case, with key factors like the potential value of your settlement playing a significant role in the decision-making process. It is also important to first discuss with your attorney whether pre-settlement funding is appropriate for you and your case.

### 2\. Case Evaluation

The funding company will review the specifics of your case and consult with your attorney to assess your eligibility and the potential amount of funding you may receive. Ideally, their evaluation will determine that your case is strong, holds significant value, and has a high likelihood of a successful resolution.

### 3\. Funding Approval

Application approvals are processed quickly. Primecare Financial takes pride in its fast evaluations, with most decisions made within 24 to 48 hours.

### 4\. Pre-Settlement Funding Agreement

Once an application is approved, the final step before disbursing funds is for Primecare Financial to secure signatures from both the plaintiff and their attorney. This pre-settlement funding agreement outlines the terms, conditions, payment schedule, and rates. Once the documents are signed and everything is in order, the funds are released.

### 5\. Pre-Settlement Funding Process

Primecare Financial frequently offers same-day pre-settlement funding, with the funds typically delivered via Western Union, direct deposit or check.

**Is There a Pre-settlement Limit?**

Primecare Financial determines pre-settlement funding limits based on a range of factors related to both the case and the plaintiff, including:

  * Likelihood of success in the case

**Can I Get More Than One Pre-Settlement Funding Disbursement?**

In some cases, it’s possible to secure multiple pre-settlement disbursements, although this can vary based on the specifics of the case. Additional funding may be requested for reasons like:

  * Misjudging your financial requirements

  * Increased loss of wages
  * Unforeseen medical costs

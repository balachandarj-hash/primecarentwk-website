**General Negligence vs. Premises Liability**  
General negligence applies when someone’s careless actions cause harm—like in car accidents or medical errors. Premises liability is more specific and involves injuries due to unsafe property conditions (like slip-and-fall accidents). Both may qualify for pre-settlement cash advances.

**Do Burn Injuries Qualify?**  
Yes. Burn injuries caused by someone’s failure to act responsibly—like improper electrical maintenance or negligence by a business—are considered general negligence. If you’ve suffered such an injury, **PrimeCare Network** can help you apply for funding while your legal claim progresses.

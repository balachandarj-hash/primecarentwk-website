Pre-settlement funding provides plaintiffs in personal injury lawsuits with the financial support they need while awaiting case resolution.

It’s a non-recourse funding option, meaning repayment is only required if you win or settle your case, making it an attractive alternative for those facing financial hardship during litigation.

### **Who Benefits from Pre-Settlement Funding?**

Plaintiffs facing financial distress during extended legal proceedings often turn to pre-settlement funding to cover daily living or medical expenses. For example, imagine Jane, a single mother recovering from a car accident in New York. With limited income due to her injuries, pre-settlement funding offers her the means to manage expenses without the burden of immediate repayment.

### **How Does Pre-Settlement Funding Work in New York?**

Pre-settlement funding in New York follows a simple process:

  1. **Application:** Submit details about your case, including relevant documents like medical records and accident reports. Your attorney will assist in verifying the case details.
  2. **Evaluation:** The funding company reviews the strength of your case, the damages incurred, and applicable insurance coverage.
  3. **Approval:** If approved, funding is typically provided within 24-48 hours.
  4. **Repayment:** Repayment is made from your settlement proceeds after your case concludes.

### **Importance of Pre-Settlement Funding in New York**

With New York’s high cost of living—30% above the national average—pre-settlement funding offers critical financial relief. Unlike loans, pre-settlement funding doesn’t require monthly payments and ensures you aren’t pressured to settle for less than your case’s value.

### **Types of Cases Eligible for Pre-Settlement Funding**

Pre-settlement funding isn’t limited to car accidents. It applies to various personal injury cases, including:

  * **Premises Negligence (Slip & Fall):** Injuries from unsafe conditions in public spaces.
  * **Construction Negligence:** Accidents caused by unsafe practices on construction sites.
  * **Civil Rights Violations:** Breaches of fair treatment due to discrimination.
  * **Maritime and Railroad Injuries:** Covered under federal statutes like the Jones Act and FELA.
  * **Wrongful Death Claims:** Support for families seeking justice for a loved one’s loss.

### **Advantages of Pre-Settlement Funding**

  1. **Non-Recourse:** No repayment is required unless your case is successful.
  2. **Quick Access:** Funds are disbursed rapidly—often within days of approval.
  3. **No Credit Check:** Your case's merit, not your credit score, determines eligibility.
  4. **Financial Relief:** Enables plaintiffs to manage essential expenses like rent, medical bills, and groceries without additional debt.

### **Key Considerations**

While pre-settlement funding provides significant benefits, it’s important to:

  * Understand the funding terms, including fees and repayment amounts.
  * Consult with your attorney to determine if this option aligns with your case strategy.

### **Conclusion**

Pre-settlement funding can provide a financial safety net for those navigating the complexities of a personal injury lawsuit. Whether you’re dealing with an auto accident, a slip-and-fall case, or other negligence claims, pre-settlement funding offers plaintiffs the resources they need to focus on recovery and achieving a fair settlement.

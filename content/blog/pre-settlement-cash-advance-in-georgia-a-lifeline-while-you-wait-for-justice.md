### What Is Pre-Settlement Funding?

Pre-settlement funding, sometimes called a lawsuit loan (though technically different), offers upfront cash to plaintiffs in active personal injury cases. **Unlike a traditional loan, it’s non-recourse** —you repay only if your case settles or wins. If you don’t receive a settlement, you owe nothing.

There are:

  * **No monthly payments**

  * **No upfront costs**

  * **No risk if you lose your case**

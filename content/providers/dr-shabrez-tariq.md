Dr. Shabrez Tariq has been practicing in the Houston area for last for seven years. He has performed thousands of injections/procedures on people with low back pain, neck pain, and joint pain. He is passionate about helping people regain the physical and psychological health that these painful conditions take away. Dr. Tariq currently serves as the Adjunct faculty with UT- Memorial Hermann TIRR and is also the Medical Director of Houston Spine and Joint Pain Consultants. Dr. Tariq completed his internship in General Surgery at Union Memorial Hospital; in Baltimore and his residency in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation at the prestigious Baylor College of Medicine. . (Pain Doctor Houston tx)
He went on to complete his Interventional Pain and Spine fellowship at Pain and Spine Consultants, Nashville, TN where he trained in invasive therapeutic and diagnostic procedures in treating back and neck pain. He is also trained in Ultrasound-guided musculoskeletal and joint injections. Dr. Tariq’s area of interest is in the treatment of back, neck, chronic spine-related, and fibromyalgia pain management.
Dr. Tariq earned his medical and post graduate degree from J N Medical College-Aligarh; India. He worked as a General Surgeon from 2003 until his move to the United States in 2006.

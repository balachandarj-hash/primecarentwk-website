Double board certified in psychiatry and internal medicine and certified in integrative medicine, Dr. Silvertooth takes vested interest in treating every patient holistically. She has been in practice since 2004 after finishing her psychiatry and internal medicine residency at Duke University Medical Center in North Carolina. She currently has a private practice in the West Lake Hills area as well as serving as Sports Psychiatry Consultant for the University of Texas and serving on the board of directors for Texas Integrative Health.

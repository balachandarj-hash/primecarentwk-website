Dr. Julian Osuji is a Clinical Neuropsychologist and a Licensed Psychologist in the State of Texas. He received his PhD in Clinical Psychology from Saint Louis University in 2003 and completed a 2-year post-doctoral fellowship in Neuropsychology at UT Southwestern Medical Center at Dallas in 2005 prior to joining their Faculty.
In addition to holding a faculty appointment at the medical center, Dr. Osuji has specialized in the treatment of Mood Disorders and the evaluation of acquired brain injuries during the course of his career. He has since worked with a wide array of patients from diverse backgrounds in psychotherapy and other treatments of Mood Disorders, and has performed hundreds of Neuropsychological evaluations during the course of his career.
He has worked extensively with American veterans at the VA Hospital in Dallas and in various other settings. He has also worked with a diverse group of patients during his time as the primary Neuropsychologist at Presbyterian Hospital in Dallas
Dr. Osuji also has experience working with Professional Sports teams in Dallas, TX. Mainly working directly with athletes who have sustained injuries to the head, but also helping athletes to optimize their performance on the field.
He currently serves as the Clinical Director of Dallas Neurorehab Center, where he has assembled a strong professional team and clinical staff to provide the best medical care for his patients.

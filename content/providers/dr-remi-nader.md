Dr.Remi Nader is the Founder and President of the International Center for Neuroscience. He is a Board-Certified fellowship-trained Neurosurgeon. A proud member of American Association of Neurological Surgeons, the Congress of Neurological Surgeons and the North American Spine Society Dr Nader treats all aspects of spinal and neurologic diseases including degenerative and traumatic conditions of the cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine. He is a leader in the field of minimally invasive spine surgery.

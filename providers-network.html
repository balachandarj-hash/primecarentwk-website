Providers Network

A nationwide clinical network built for personal injury—so patients get timely treatment, providers get predictable partnership, and attorneys get a clear path to care.

Why the network matters

The right clinician, at the right time—without settlement delays.

PrimeCare Network partners with hospitals, surgery centers, specialists, imaging centers, chiropractic and therapy clinics, emergency programs, and brain trauma centers across the United States.

Through NorthstarDoc connectivity and our care teams, referrals land with providers prepared to treat PI patients—while lien financing and receivables management remove the cash-flow friction that often stalls care.

How the network works

The right access for the injury, location, and requested services.

Personal injury treatment rarely happens in one place. A patient may need an initial evaluation, diagnostic imaging, specialty care, therapy, procedures, or follow-up treatment—often across multiple offices.

PrimeCare provides access to an established network of healthcare providers, facilities, and professional resources. Our team helps identify appropriate network options based on the patient’s needs, injury, location, and requested services.

PrimeCare coordinates access to independent providers and professionals. PrimeCare does not itself render the medical, clinical, diagnostic, rehabilitation, or professional services listed on this site.

How referrals work

From request to the right network option.

Finding the right provider is often the first step in a longer treatment journey. Once network access is identified, Care Coordination can help manage appointments, imaging, specialists, records, cost estimates, and updates through one point of contact.

  1. 01

    Request

    An attorney, patient, or participating provider contacts PrimeCare with the patient’s treatment needs.

  2. 02

    Match

    Our team reviews the request and identifies appropriate network resources based on injury, location, and requested services.

  3. 03

    Connect

    We help connect the patient with the selected independent provider, facility, or professional resource.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Care Coordination can continue supporting appointments, status updates, records, and related administrative needs.

Who the network serves

Clearer access for attorneys, patients, and providers.

For attorneys

Locate appropriate providers and facilities for personal injury clients without building every referral pathway from scratch. Pair network access with Care Coordination for ongoing visibility into treatment progress.

For patients

Get connected to independent clinicians and facilities suited to the injury and location—then stay informed as appointments and next steps are coordinated.

For providers

Participate in a more connected referral process. PrimeCare helps route coordinated personal injury referrals and reduce unnecessary administrative back-and-forth with law firms and patients.

For participating providers

A clearer administrative connection around each referral.

PrimeCare helps participating providers receive coordinated personal injury referrals while creating a clearer connection between the provider, patient, and law firm. Our team can assist with referral coordination, patient scheduling, appointment confirmations, follow-up communication, medical record requests, cost estimate coordination, law firm communication, and reduction coordination when applicable.
